About ArrowEscapeGame.com

ArrowEscapeGame.com is a small independent site built around one idea: a great puzzle should take five seconds to start and five minutes to put down (good luck with that last part).

The game here is Arrow Escape by Bravestars Games - a clean little logic puzzle where you clear a board of arrows by tapping them in the right order. Blocked arrows collide and cost you hearts, so every tap matters. It's the kind of game that looks simple on level one and quietly humbles you by level ten.

Why We Built It

We like puzzles that respect your time. No app store, no account, no tutorial walls - you open the page and you're playing. This site just gives the game a fast, clean home with some genuinely useful tips for when a board has you stuck.

How the Site Is Supported

The game is and will remain free. The site is supported by advertising served through Google AdSense.
