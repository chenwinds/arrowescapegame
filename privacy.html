Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 2026

Overview

ArrowEscapeGame.com ("we", "the site") is a free browser-based puzzle game site. We collect as little data as possible: there are no accounts, no sign-ups, and we never ask for your name or personal information to play.

Information We Do Not Collect

We do not ask for or store your name, email address, phone number, or gameplay data. Your progress in the game is handled by the embedded game itself, not by us.

Embedded Game Content

The game on this site is embedded from GameDistribution, a third-party game platform. When you play, GameDistribution may collect its own technical and gameplay data (such as device type and progress) under its own privacy policy. We recommend reviewing GameDistribution's privacy policy for details.

Cookies

This site uses cookies - small text files stored on your device - for the following purposes:

  • Analytics cookies: to understand aggregate traffic patterns (for example, which pages are visited most).
  • Advertising cookies: set by our advertising partners, including Google AdSense, to serve and measure ads.
  • Game cookies: set by the embedded game platform to remember your progress and preferences.

Third-Party Advertising (Google AdSense)

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.

You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of third-party vendor cookies at www.aboutads.info/choices.

Where required by law (including the EEA, UK, and Switzerland), we will request your consent for the use of cookies and personalized ads through a consent management platform before such cookies are set.

Log Data

Like most websites, our hosting provider may automatically record standard log information when you visit (IP address, browser type, pages visited, timestamps). This data is used only for security and operational purposes.

Children's Privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
